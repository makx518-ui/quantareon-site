Privacy Policy

Luck Forecast — QUANTAREON Labs · Last updated: 5 August 2026

Short version: Luck Forecast does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app works fully offline and has no servers of its own.

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing. Luck Forecast has no user accounts, no registration, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers of any kind.

2. Information you enter

To calculate a forecast the app asks for your date, time and place of birth, your gender and your current city. This information is:

  • processed entirely on your device;
  • stored only in the app's local storage on that device, so you do not have to type it again;
  • never uploaded, transmitted or shared with us or with anyone else.

We never see this data. Deleting the app removes it completely.

3. Location

The app offers an optional "Use my location" button. If you tap it, your device provides approximate coordinates, which are used once — on the device — to find the nearest city in the built-in offline database. The coordinates are not stored and not transmitted anywhere. You can ignore the button entirely and select your country and city by hand; the app works exactly the same way.

4. Internet access

Luck Forecast performs all astronomical and calendar calculations locally and requires no internet connection to function. The app does not contact any server operated by us.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and contains no content intended for them. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Payments

Luck Forecast is a paid application sold through Google Play. All payment processing is handled by Google. We never receive or store your payment details.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose personal data — because we do not collect any.

8. Your rights

Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You remain in full control: clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything you entered.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
